Resonance Rx

AI-personalized music therapy for dementia care

The Problem

Music outlasts memory. Dementia systematically dismantles cognitive function, but the brain's response to familiar music stays remarkably intact, making it one of the most powerful and most underused tools in dementia care.

Yet music therapy has remained largely inaccessible, inconsistent, and impossible to measure at the scale caregivers actually need it.

Resonance Rx moves music therapy into the modern era: AI identifies which songs hold the deepest meaning for each patient, layering therapeutic audio underneath to measurably reduce agitation and anxiety, delivered wherever the patient is.

01

Finding the activation gap

Medicare reimbursement requires consistent adherence across a 30-day period. Early data showed engagement stuck around 25%. I defined and tracked engagement metrics, identified the gap, and hypothesised that patients lacked a lightweight ritual to bring them back between sessions. I proposed and A/B tested a Mood Diary feature — a low-friction way to log emotional state before and after sessions. Engagement lifted substantially, crossing the threshold that made Medicare-eligible adherence achievable.

02

Designing for two very different worlds

The product had to work for a caregiver administering a session during facility rounds, and for a patient in their 70s or 80s navigating it alone at home on their own device. Migrating from a tablet-based deployment to a PWA unlocked both: onboarding dropped from days to roughly five minutes, and a use case we had not fully anticipated opened up — patients using the product independently, without any caregiver present.

03

Co-designing care inside the facility

Not every resident could operate a device. During work with a senior living facility in Texas, I identified a segment we had not designed for — device-less residents who relied entirely on staff for daily care. Rather than treating this as an edge case, I embedded with the facility team to co-design a staff-mediated workflow: caregivers administer therapy during daily rounds through the provider dashboard, no additional equipment or clinical training required. I ran usability testing across all three personas — patients, caregivers, and providers — to validate the workflow and surface friction before it reached the field.

04

The clinical infrastructure that made it viable

I built and iterated the provider dashboard used across 32 providers — clinics, senior living facilities, and independent practices. Every design decision had a compliance dimension: PHI-compliant data capture, reimbursement-ready reporting structured for Medicare billing, and remote mood monitoring between care visits. I worked with regulatory consultants to understand the requirements, then owned translating them into product.
